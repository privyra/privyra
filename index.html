Four instruments

A closed book still needs a way to move.

Each instrument is its own page. Nothing here scrolls you down a single long sheet.

INK

Ink transfer

Pay a person, not a permanent address. The chain sees a one-time seal. Only the recipient can recognize it.

  • One payment, one address
  • Native coin or a token
  • You sign, or nothing moves

BOOK

Quiet swap

Trade from a fresh seal so a pool learns a new address, not the wallet you use everywhere else.

  • Slippage you set
  • Route shown, or “unset”
  • No invented price

FERRY

The ferry

Cross from Robinhood Chain to Elysium, or back. Arrive in the open, or ask for a seal on the far shore.

  • Two real networks
  • Optional private arrival
  • Receipts you can check

CLERKS

Night clerks

Small readers that live in this tab. One watches the chain. One holds the equity catalogue. One waits for a live spread.

  • No server-side agent
  • Preferences stay local
  • The ledger clerk is already reading

How a letter leaves the desk

Three motions, then the chain.

Connect

Use the wallet already in your browser. MetaMask, Rabby, and any other EIP-1193 injection. Privyra can add Robinhood Chain for you. It cannot sign on your behalf.

Seal

When the registry is published, one signature derives a receiving seal in the page. Until that contract exists, the transfer page shows the shape of the letter and does not invent an address.

Send

Transfer, swap, or cross. The wallet shows the transaction. You approve it, or you don’t. The only record that matters is the one the chain keeps.

Wax on the letter

Hold more. The clerks carry more.

In the old houses, the weight of wax on a letter decided how many notes went out after dark. When $PRIVYRA is issued on Robinhood Chain, the balance in your connected wallet will set a daily allowance of sealed actions: an ink transfer, a quiet swap, or a ferry crossing. Nothing is metered today. The allowance contract is unset.

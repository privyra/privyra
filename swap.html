Quiet swap

Trade from a seal, not from the wallet the pool already knows.

A quiet swap is a trade whose sender is a one-time seal. The pool learns a fresh address. Your everyday wallet stays off the ticket. The form below is the desk. It will not ask your wallet to sign until a router and a quoter exist, and it will not print a price in their place.

Why the receive field is empty

A rate without a pool would be fiction.

Privyra will quote from the chain: reserves in, fee out, minimum received after your slippage. contracts.swapRouter and contracts.quoter in js/config.js are empty, so this page refuses the last step.

Balances are different. If your wallet is connected, Ether is read with eth_getBalance on the shore you picked, and a token is read with balanceOf once its address is filled in. A failed read says n/a. It does not become zero.

Max copies the balance into the amount. A live swap would keep a little of the native coin for gas. That reserve is not subtracted here, because nothing is sent.

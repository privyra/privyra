What deposit will mean

You would fund a pair, not a promise.

Adding liquidity sends two amounts into a pool contract and gives you a claim on the fees that pair earns. Privyra will show the share of the pool those amounts represent. Today the share is a dash, because there is no pool to divide.

The shore selector only changes where a balance is read. Ether on Robinhood Chain and HYPE on Elysium are native. USDC and $PRIVYRA stay “contract unset” until their addresses are written in the config.

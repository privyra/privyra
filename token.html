House rules, not a meter

Five weights of wax.

These tiers are the custom the desk will keep. They are not read from a contract, because contracts.allowance is empty. A connected wallet cannot be told its tier yet. When the token and the allowance contract are both set, the page will call balanceOf and match the row. Until then it will not guess your holding.

Intended cut of the supply

Written as a proposal, not as an allocation that exists on a chain.

  • 55% — community liquidity and the sealed-action pool.
  • 20% — desk treasury, for audits and the public RPC bill.
  • 15% — contributors, with a four-year vest once a vesting contract exists.
  • 10% — the partnership shore, for integrations that publish their own clerks.

No figure above is a balance. Do not treat it as one.

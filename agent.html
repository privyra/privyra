Privyra AI

A clerk that stays in the page.

Ask how a quiet swap, a ferry crossing, or an ink transfer works. Ask what the wax tiers require. Ask for a close the markets page already has. Or tell it to open a page. The button in the corner of every other page is this same clerk. Nothing here calls a model unless you later set an endpoint, and that value is never a key.

  • “How do I swap?” explains the ticket and why the receive field is empty.
  • “Open the ferry” leaves this page for the bridge.
  • “AAPL close” reads the end-of-day snapshot, with the session date.
  • “Wax tiers” lists Visitor through House from the config, and says they are not enforced.

Night clerks, on the Agents page, watch the chain. This clerk talks. They do not share a memory, and neither one holds a seed.
